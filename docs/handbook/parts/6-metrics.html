Not measured

  • Anything about pooling at volume: orders per trip, kilometres per order with and without pooling, minutes from order to door. These need a day of many orders, which is not built (section 5).
  • A trip of several stops, end to end. Tests cover it; the end-to-end run has one order.
  • Load on the new code, and load beyond one machine.
  • The app's speed on a phone, and an order placed from the installed app.
  • Real picking and riding speeds. Every measured run is scripted.
  • Trim and rejection on real lots. Every lot weighed so far is a script's or a demo farm's: the tomatoes pictured in section 3, and 20 kg of onions on 23 November (20.4 kg arrived, 0.3 kg turned away, 20 packs of 1 kg, 0.1 kg trim).
