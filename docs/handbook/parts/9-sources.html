The project's own documents

  • docs/DESIGN.md, 24 November 2026: every decision in section 2 with what was considered and not taken, what it costs, and its own list of outside sources.
  • docs/journal/: one entry for each piece of work, with what went wrong. Five are from 24 November: orders on the server; the app on those orders and a key for map tiles; stocked shops, IIT Bhilai and India's calendar; an order reaches a door; an app that installs.
  • docs/DEMO_ON_A_PHONE.md: the run sheet behind section 6.

Outside sources the design leans on

  • B. E. Gillett and L. R. Miller, "A Heuristic Algorithm for the Vehicle-Dispatch Problem", Operations Research 22(2), 1974: the sweep method.
  • IETF, RFC 2104, "HMAC: Keyed-Hashing for Message Authentication": the door code. RFC 2606, "Reserved Top Level DNS Names": the demo farms' .invalid address.
  • H3, "Tables of cell statistics across resolutions": the size of a resolution 9 cell. h3geo.org
  • AWS, Amazon Location Service developer guide: displaying a map, and best practices for API keys.
  • Google Maps Platform, Terms of Service and Service Specific Terms: no use with a non-Google map.
  • OWASP Cheat Sheet Series, "Multifactor Authentication" and "Forgot Password": the rules a one-time code follows.
  • AWS documentation: password management for RDS with Secrets Manager (the seven-day rotation), HTTP headers of Application Load Balancers (which caller's address to trust), and the ECS deployment circuit breaker.
  • Expo, "Create a release build locally": the Android build and its debug key.
  • Swiggy Instamart's fresh vegetable listings: packs, pieces with weight ranges, bunches.
  • The AWS Price List API, read on 23 November 2026: every price in section 2.

Measured by the project: every number in section 4, each with its date and method.

How this document was made

The pictures of section 3 are saved by the script that makes the end-to-end run, scripts/record_demo.sh. It starts the backend on an empty database, adds generated sales history and address lookups so that the hub's dashboards have something to show, drives the app and the hub's pages in a browser, and saves a picture at each step. Running it again remakes them.

The long screenshot of the Bangalore shop and the picture of the installed app were taken separately, on 24 November. This report does not use the app's demo-data pictures or the old placeholder screen of the agent.

The pages are HTML in docs/handbook/, printed to PDF by build.ps1. The numbers in section 4 are typed in by hand from the runs named beside them.
