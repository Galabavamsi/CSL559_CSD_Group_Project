9 · What is leftNot built yet, in the order to build it

Ten days remain before the demo. Hubs in Bhilai and the hub's intake screen, first on this list a version ago, are built. What follows is in the order the journey needs it.

PieceWhy it is here in the orderDone when
1Customer orders in the backend; the catalogue carries grade, harvest date, farm and batch price Replaces the bridge in the app and turns the demo-data screens of section 5 real. An order is a record on the server; the shop shows a lot's grade and date.
2Delivery: pooling orders into trips, the agent's screens, the door-step code The last leg of the journey, and the part that makes it "pooled". Two orders for one area leave in one trip and are handed over against their codes.
3Produce only in the hub's demo data; rescue prices near the use-by date Bangalore's shelves still carry the dark store's groceries. The shop shows only produce; a batch near its date is cheaper.
4Stock requests to farms Closes the loop: the forecast asks farms for tomorrow's produce. A hub's shortfall becomes a request a farmer can accept.
5An installable Android build, and the phone against staging Expo Go is enough to demo; an APK is what a user installs. The app is installed from a file and runs against staging.
6A simulator and load at city scale; a production environment The numbers in section 7 come from one machine and scripted pickers. A simulated day of orders for both cities, with the KPIs it produced.

How this document was made

The screenshots and the video are produced by scripts/record_demo.sh, which starts the backend on an empty database, drives the app and the hub's pages in a browser, and records what happens. Running it again remakes them. The pages are HTML in docs/handbook/, printed to PDF by build.ps1.
