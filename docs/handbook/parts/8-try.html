6 · How to try itThe app on an Android phone, against staging

The app installs from a file and talks to the staging copy on AWS. No laptop is needed. The run below takes about eight minutes; the full run sheet is docs/DEMO_ON_A_PHONE.md.

Get the app

Download for Android:
https://github.com/Galabavamsi/CSL559_CSD_Group_Project/releases/tag/app-staging-2026-11-24

The file is Farm2Home-staging.apk: 36.8 MB, version 1.0.0, for 64-bit ARM phones.

  • Android will warn you. The app comes from outside the Play Store and is signed with a debug key. Allow your browser to install it, once, then open Farm2Home.
  • Use mobile data. Some networks block the staging address; a campus Wi-Fi did. The app then says "No connection".
  • Have two email addresses, one for the customer and one for the delivery agent. Sign-in is a six-digit code sent by email. A Gmail address with +agent before the @ is a second address that arrives in the same inbox.
  • Two phones if you can. With one, you sign out and in between customer and agent, and each sign-in is one more code. The agent stays on duty while signed out.

The run

WhoDoWhat it shows
1AgentSign in, choosing Delivery. Pick Bhilai, the hub BH-04 IIT Bhilai, then Go on duty. Agents belong to a hub. The one free longest gets the next trip.
2CustomerSign in. Choose Bhilai, then Kumhari, and tap Request delivery here. Then choose IIT Bhilai. Kumhari is in no zone; the request is counted when the next hub is chosen. IIT Bhilai is served by BH-04.
3CustomerLook at the shop. A photograph, a grade, the day it was picked and a use-by date on every card. Spinach is marked down.
4CustomerAdd three or four items, spinach among them. View cart, Hold my items, type an address, Pay. The units are off sale for everyone else for ten minutes. The total is the hub's own bill. No money moves.
5CustomerStay on the order's screen. By staging's settings it should read Packed, then Pooled, within about a minute and a half, with four digits.
6AgentCollect 1 bag: tap Bag checked. On the stop, type the customer's four digits and tap Hand over. The customer's order turns Out for delivery. A wrong code is counted, and five lock the hand-over.
7CustomerOpen the order.Delivered, with the time of every step.

What staging does for you

SettingWhat it doesWhat you would otherwise need
Demo farmsEvery half hour, a hub nearly out of an item gets a lot from a demo farm, through the ordinary intakeA farmer listing and a receiver weighing
Demo hubAn order no picker starts within 45 seconds is picked and packed by the server, as a picker wouldSomeone at the picker's page
Short waitA lone packed order leaves after half a minute, not tenThree more orders, or ten minutes
Agents sign upAnyone may sign in as a delivery agent and choose a hubThe platform team making the account

The lots, the picking and the trip on staging are real records made by the real code. What these four settings simulate is the people. Staging keeps what earlier runs did; nothing needs resetting.

If something goes wrong

You seeLikely causeDo
"No connection"The phone is on a Wi-Fi that blocks stagingSwitch to mobile data
No code arrivesMore than one a minute, or too many in an hourWait a minute; check spam
The shop is emptyThe hub ran out between two rounds of the demo farmsWait for the half hour, or choose another area
The order stays at PackedNo agent is on duty at that hubGo on duty as the agent, at the order's hub
The agent sees no tripThe order is from another hubChoose the hub the customer's screen names

What to say is not built

  • A map, the agent's position, and a time of arrival. Distances are straight lines times 1.7 for now.
  • Real payment.
  • What the hub does with a bag that comes back.
  • Hubs asking farms for what they expect to sell.

To see two stops on one trip, place two orders from different areas of one hub before the agent goes on duty.
