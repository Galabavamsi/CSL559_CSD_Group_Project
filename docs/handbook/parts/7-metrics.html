Does batch picking save walking at peak?48% less walking30 paid orders, all waiting. One at a time: 30 walks, 622 bin-steps. In batches of up to 3: 12 walks, 322 steps. A walk costs twice the furthest bin it visits.
Does slotting by sales save walking?1% overall; 8% for orders of ambient items only200 random orders drawn by 14 days of sales, before and after the plan. Chilled and frozen bins sit at the far end of the walk whatever the plan, so most orders gain nothing.

What the system measures about itself

The ops dashboard shows each KPI of the design beside its target, per hub:

KPITarget
Order to packed (90th percentile)≤ 120 s
Fill rate≥ 99% of units ordered
Picker productivity≥ 100 items per picker-hour
Inventory accuracy≥ 99.5% of counted lines
Stock-out rate≤ 2% of carried items
Wastage≤ 1% of sales value
Forecast error (WAPE)≤ 30%
Event relay lag≤ 30 s

The app also serves Prometheus metrics, with alert rules written for them:

AlertFires when
Failed holds spikeover 20% of holds refused for 5 minutes
Slow reads99th percentile of a read above 100 ms
Events stuckthe oldest unsent event is over 30 s old
Hub silentno event from a hub for 15 minutes

Every request carries an id that appears on each log line it causes, and every staff action is written to an audit log with who, what and the answer they got.

Not measured yet: trim and rejection across many lots, anything about delivery, the app's speed on a phone, and load beyond one machine.
