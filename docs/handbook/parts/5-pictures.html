3 · The system in picturesThe farmer

A farmer signs in with a code sent by email, registers a farm where they stand, and lists what was picked. The backend finds the nearest hub with the right shelves and room for the weight.

Not built for the farmer: payment for the accepted weight, a photograph of the lot, and requests from a hub for what it expects to sell. The asking price is recorded; it does not yet set the price on the shelf.
