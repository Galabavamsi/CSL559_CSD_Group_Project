1 · Where it standsWhat this is, and what works today

A farmer lists a lot in kilograms. A local hub weighs it, grades it and packs it into the units customers buy. A customer's phone shows only what the hub serving their address can deliver now. A delivery agent takes several bags out in one trip and hands each over against a code.

On 24 November 2026

  • The whole journey runs on the real backend. Delivery, the last step, was built on 24 November.
  • The backend is one FastAPI application with a PostgreSQL database, on AWS in Mumbai. The copy people can use is called staging. It runs the main branch at merge commit 567ff67 (pull request 100).
  • One phone app serves customers, farmers and delivery agents, and now installs on an Android phone from a file. Hub staff use web pages.
  • 800 automated tests pass on AWS for that commit.
  • Two cities, six hubs: 2 in Bangalore and 4 in Bhilai, the fourth beside IIT Bhilai, where the demo will be given.

New since the build of 23 November

  • An order is a record on the server. It was kept on the phone.
  • The shop says which batch a pack comes from: grade, harvest date, use-by date and farm.
  • A batch in its last day is sold 30% cheaper, and the checkout shows the hub's own bill.
  • Delivery: trips, the agent's screens, a four-digit code at the door.
  • A customer outside every delivery zone can ask for delivery, and the request is counted.
  • The calendar is India's: a fault found on staging at one in the morning.

Five things a reader should be told first

  • Payment is a stand-in. Tapping Pay confirms the order and no money moves.
  • On staging, some people are simulated. Demo farms deliver a lot when a hub is nearly out of an item, and an order no picker starts within 45 seconds is picked and packed by the server, through the code a person's screen calls.
  • The pictures in section 3 are from a scripted run on the project's development server, not from staging: the real backend code, a real PostgreSQL, and the app's web build at phone size.
  • The installed Android app has been started on one phone and showed its sign-in screen. Signing in and ordering from it had not been done when this was written.
  • Not built: a map, the agent's position, a time of arrival, what the hub does with a bag that comes back, hubs asking farms for produce, a simulated day of many orders. Section 5 has the whole list.
