These screens are finished and wait for the backend. What they show comes from data built into the app: a hold that expires, orders that move through their stages by the clock, lots that get graded after a while. None of it reaches a server.

The delivery agent Not built

An agent can sign in, and that is all. An agent's account is created by the platform team and never by signing up, because an agent sees customers' addresses. The trips, the route, the hand-over against the customer's code and live location are the next large piece of work.

How the app is made

  • One app, three roles. The account's role decides the home screen.
  • One interface, two back ends. Every screen talks to one typed client. Given the server's address it is the real API; without one it is the built-in demo data. That is why unfinished parts of the backend already have screens.
  • Material Design 3, light and dark, one open-licence typeface, an original logo, and a real photograph for each kind of produce, all openly licensed.
  • The session is kept in the phone's secure store and renewed before it runs out.
