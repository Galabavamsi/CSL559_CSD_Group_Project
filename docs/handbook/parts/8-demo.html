What a live demo from Bhilai still needs

  • Staging on this version. Bhilai's three hubs and the intake page arrive with the next deploy; until then staging has hubs in Bangalore only, and a lot listed from Bhilai is refused.
  • A rehearsal on the phone. The recording ran the same app and the same backend code on the development server. Walk through way B once before the day.
  • A GPS fix good to 50 m for the farm. A phone rarely gets one indoors: register the farm outside, or by a window, before the audience arrives.

B. The live walk, about ten minutes

Start the app from the mobile folder and scan the QR code with Expo Go:

$env:EXPO_PUBLIC_API_URL = "https://staging.farm2home.galabavamsi.com"; npx.cmd expo start --clear
WhoDoSay
1Farmer, phoneSign in as Farmer. Open the farm. List produce: Tomato, 40 kg, ₹18, harvested today. "No password: a code by email. The system chose the nearest hub with room for 40 kg, and a slot tomorrow morning."
2Receiver, laptop or a second phoneOpen /intake.html and choose the hub the farmer was given. Weigh: 39.5 arrived, 1.5 turned away, grade A. Record and pack. In the bin. "The hub weighs and grades. 38 kg becomes 76 packs of 500 g, and they are on sale the moment they are in their bin."
3Farmer, phonePull down to refresh My listings."The farmer sees the grade and what was turned away. That record follows the farm to its next delivery."
4Customer, phoneAccount, sign out. Sign in as Customer with the second inbox. Choose Bhilai, then an area that hub serves. Add two Tomato. View cart. Hold my items. "The shop shows only what this hub can deliver now. Holding sets two packs aside for ten minutes: whole cart or nothing."
5Customer, phoneType an address. Pay."Payment is a stand-in; no money moves. The order now waits for a picker."
6Picker, laptop or a second phoneOpen /picker.html, choose the same hub. Get next order. Scan all. Mark packed. "The picker is sent to the bin holding the oldest batch. Every unit is scanned. Only at packing does stock on hand go down."
7Customer, phoneOpen Orders, then the order."Packed. From here it would be pooled with its neighbours into one trip. That is what we build next."

Before the day

  • Use mobile data. The campus and office networks tried so far block the site's new domain. Put the laptop on the phone's hotspot and check that https://staging.farm2home.galabavamsi.com/health answers.
  • Install Expo Go on the Android phone.
  • Fetch the staff password for the hub's pages from AWS Secrets Manager (farm2home/staging/staff-password). The built-in password is refused on a hosted site; the pages ask for the real one once.
  • Have an inbox open on the phone. The sign-in code arrives by email in a few seconds, is good for 10 minutes, and can be asked for once a minute.
  • Do not press "Reset demo data" on the hub desk on staging: it wipes everything back to the starting data.
  • Sign in to AWS only if you mean to deploy. The demo itself needs no AWS session.

The same receiver's step from a command line

If the intake page cannot be used, scripts/hub_intake.py does the same for the lot that has waited longest at the hub whose number is given:

$env:STAFF_PASSWORD = "the staff password"
py scripts\hub_intake.py --url https://staging.farm2home.galabavamsi.com --store 3 --received 39.5 --rejected 1.5 --grade A

C. The app on demo data

From the mobile folder, with no address set:

npx.cmd expo start --clear

Any email signs in and the code is 123456. Nothing leaves the phone. Choose Bhilai, Supela. Show a rescue deal, a product's grade and harvest date, checkout, and the order moving to Out for delivery with its door-step code. Say plainly that this is demo data: these are the screens, not yet the system.

If something goes wrong

What you seeWhat to do
The site does not load, or "blocked by policy"The network blocks the domain. Switch to mobile data.

No code in the inboxCheck spam. Wait a minute and ask again; five an hour per address.
"No paid orders are waiting" on the picker pageThe order is unpaid, or for another hub: check the hub chosen at the top.
"No hub within 60 km" when listingStaging is not on this version yet, or the farm was registered somewhere else.
